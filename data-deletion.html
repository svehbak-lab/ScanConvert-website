CurrenTag — Delete your data

This page explains how to ask for deletion of data related to the CurrenTag app (Android and iOS).

What data CurrenTag stores

CurrenTag has no user accounts. Scans, conversions, history and settings are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.

To manage subscriptions, our payment provider RevenueCat stores an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (products, dates, subscription status). It does not include your name or email address.

How to delete your data

  1. Data on your phone: uninstall CurrenTag. This removes all history and settings immediately.
  2. Purchase data: send an email to svehbak@gmail.com with the subject "CurrenTag data deletion" and include your order number (Google Play: starts with GPA., found in the purchase receipt email; App Store: the order ID from your Apple receipt), so we can find your anonymous record.

What is deleted and what is kept

We delete the anonymous customer record and purchase history held by RevenueCat within 30 days of your request. Deleting this data does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it first in Google Play or the App Store.

Google and Apple keep their own records of the transaction under their privacy policies; we cannot delete those.
